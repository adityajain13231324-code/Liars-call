How to play

  1. Everyone gets 5 cards. Each round has a table card: Raja, Rani or Ikka. Jokers count as the table card too.
  2. On your turn, play 1 to 3 cards face down and claim they're all the table card. You can lie.
  3. Or shout LIAR! on the player before you. Their cards get flipped.
  4. If they lied, they eat a golgappa. If they told the truth, you eat one.
  5. Everyone has 6 golgappas and two are filled with ghost-pepper pani. Eat it and you're out. Each one you survive makes the next more dangerous.
  6. When only one player still has cards, they must call LIAR. Last one standing wins.
  7. Watch the uncles and aunties: bots often give away a bluff with a tell (moustache twirl, glasses push, a flex...). Not always.
